Good to know

Straight answers.

Do I need experience to rent?
Yes — prior skid steer or track loader experience, and operators must be 21+. It protects you, the machine, and the next renter. Details on the Requirements page.
What does delivery actually look like?
Our flatbed arrives at the start of your window and sets the T64 exactly where you want it — flat, firm ground our truck can reach. When your rental ends, we come back and haul it away. You never tow anything.
What if I need to cancel?
Cancel 48+ hours before delivery for a full refund minus a $50 booking fee. Inside 48 hours, 50% is refunded. Weather that makes the work unsafe? Free reschedule or full refund — no fee kept.
