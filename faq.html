answered.

The most common things people ask before renting the T64. Not finding your answer? Email us at seanbusby10@gmail.com.

How do I book?

Click "Reserve" in the nav. Pick your dates, select attachments, and pay online — rental, delivery, and sales tax are all shown before you pay. The $600 refundable damage deposit is collected at delivery (card, cash, or check), not online. We confirm within 24 hours.

What counts as "prior skid steer experience"?

You've operated a skid steer or compact track loader before — not just watched someone else. This could be from work, a prior rental, military service, farm experience, etc. The intake form asks you to describe it briefly. We're not looking for certifications, just honest experience.

What if I damage the machine?

The $600 refundable deposit covers most normal wear-and-tear issues and small incidents. Significant damage is billed at actual repair cost. Document the machine's condition at delivery and before return — photos on your phone are fine.

How does delivery work?

Our flatbed arrives at your site at the start of your rental window and drops the T64 where you want it (within reason — flat, firm ground, accessible to our truck). At the end, we come back and pick it up. You do not tow, trailer, or transport the machine under any circumstance.

How much is delivery?

Three zones based on distance from Alexandria, VA (22310): Zone 1 (0–20 mi) $275 · Zone 2 (21–35 mi) $400 · Zone 3 (36–50 mi) $525. Use the calculator on our Delivery Zones page to check your ZIP.

What about fuel?

The T64 takes diesel (28.3-gal tank). You return it at the same fuel level we delivered it — usually full. Diesel stations are plentiful and any marked "off-road diesel" or "#2 diesel" is fine.

What if it rains?

The T64 is designed to work in weather. Your rental clock doesn't stop because of rain. That said, if your job site turns into a mud pit, the rubber tracks can still operate but you'll want to think about whether you should. Don't try to work in standing water.

Can I add attachments mid-rental?

Attachments are selected at booking so we can pre-load them on the delivery. Add-ons later would require a separate trip, which isn't something we're currently offering.

Can I rent longer than a week?

Not at this time — the longest flat-rate option is the 7-day weekly. If you have a bigger project in mind, email us directly and we'll see what's possible.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel 48+ hours before your delivery day — full refund minus a $50 booking fee. Inside 48 hours — 50% refund. No-show or same-day cancellation — no refund. If weather makes delivery or safe operation impossible, we offer a free reschedule or a full refund with no fee kept.

Do you work weekends & holidays?

Weekend rentals (Fri–Sun) are our most popular — that's a core product. Holiday availability can be tight; book early.

Can someone else on my team operate the machine?

One listed operator per rental. That person must meet the 21+ and prior-experience requirements. Don't hand the keys to anyone not on the booking.
