Before You Book

Rental
requirements.

These aren't formalities — they protect you, us, and the job site. Every renter meets every item on this list.

Age 21+
Operator must be at least 21 years old. Valid ID required at delivery.
Prior experience required
You've operated a skid steer or compact track loader before — this is not a training machine, no exceptions.
$600 refundable deposit
Collected at booking. Returned in full after pickup, assuming the machine comes back the way it left.
Delivery-only · no customer pickup
We drop off and pick up — an insurance requirement, firm. We can't help if you want to trailer it yourself.
48-hour advance booking
Same-day rentals aren't possible — book at least two days out so we can route delivery.
Site must be accessible
Our delivery truck needs reasonable access. No extreme grades, soft mud, or locked gates without a key.

What happens if something goes wrong?

The $600 deposit covers most minor damage — scratched paint, bent pins, lost pins, small dents. Anything beyond that is billed at actual repair cost. The renter is responsible for the machine from delivery to pickup. Fuel it up before return (takes diesel, 28.3 gal tank). Don't let anyone outside your listed operator run it.

72 hours — that's the rule.

Cancel 72+ hours before your rental start and you get a full deposit refund. Inside 72 hours, the deposit is forfeited (we've already blocked your dates and turned away other customers). Reschedules inside the window are accommodated when we can.
